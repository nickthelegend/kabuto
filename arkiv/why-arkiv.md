# Why Arkiv

An intent is a temporary public offer. Arkiv's indexed attributes let fillers filter by status, chain, deadline, and trusted owner. Its expiry matches the signed deadline, so stale listings leave the board automatically. Fills become a separately queryable public outcome log for 30 days. These are data functions: Kabuto's authorization, price constraints, and settlement remain on chain 1337.

The user-facing Intents tab reads this state directly from Rabdi. The raw judge query is in [`schema.md`](schema.md).
